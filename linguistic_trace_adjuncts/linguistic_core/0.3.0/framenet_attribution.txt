This adjunct contains a deterministic source-native projection of FrameNet
frames, frame elements, lexical-unit declarations, and frame relations for
inspection. It does not contain the raw FrameNet archive, FrameNet documents,
or lexical-unit annotation corpora. Predicate-to-FrameNet links remain donor
candidate alignments unless a separate governed review verifies them.
